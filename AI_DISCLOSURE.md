# AI Use Disclosure

ProofLayer was developed with assistance from AI coding and reasoning tools.

## Tools used

- **Claude** — application architecture, implementation, code generation, debugging, deployment assistance, and documentation.
- **ChatGPT** — architecture planning, technical reasoning, debugging, documentation, submission preparation, and presentation planning.
- **Grok** — assistance with Bright Data Scraper Studio configuration and troubleshooting.

## Human contribution

The team contributed the project concept, product direction, hackathon strategy, scraper configuration, account setup, credential management, testing decisions, review of generated work, and final presentation.

The team is responsible for understanding the submitted architecture and technical decisions and will explain the system during judging.

## Transparency

AI assistance was used as a development aid and is disclosed in accordance with the hackathon rules. The team did not intentionally present AI-generated work as independently authored human code.
